  <p>

    The Spot Sober Lounge respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy

    explains how The Spot Sober Lounge mobile application (the “App”)

    handles information on iOS and Android.

  </p>


  <h2>Information We Collect</h2>


  <p>

    The App does not require users to create an account and does not

    directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise

    location, payment-card information, or other personally identifiable

    information through the App.

  </p>


  <p>

    The App does not include third-party advertising, behavioral tracking,

    or analytics SDKs.

  </p>


  <h2>App Content and Network Access</h2>


  <p>

    The App connects to The Spot Sober Lounge website to retrieve and

    display current public information, including meetings, events,

    merchandise, and membership information.

  </p>


  <p>

    When the App requests this content, the website or its hosting

    providers may automatically process routine technical information

    such as an IP address, request time, requested page or resource, and

    basic device or network information for content delivery, reliability,

    and security.

  </p>


  <p>

    The App does not use this information to build user profiles or for

    advertising.

  </p>


  <h2>Local Caching, Retention, and Deletion</h2>


  <p>

    To improve loading speed and allow previously retrieved content to

    remain available when the website is slow or temporarily unavailable,

    the App may cache public meetings, events, product information, and

    product images locally on the user’s device.

  </p>


  <p>

    This local cache contains public website content rather than

    information submitted by the user. Cached content remains on the

    device until it is updated, the App’s stored data is cleared, or the

    App is uninstalled.

  </p>


  <p>

    The Spot Sober Lounge does not receive or maintain a separate copy of

    this local cache.

  </p>


  <p>

    Because the App does not provide user accounts or maintain

    app-specific personal profiles, there is no app account data to

    delete. Users may remove locally stored App data by clearing the App’s

    data through their device settings or by uninstalling the App.

  </p>


  <p>

    Privacy or deletion questions may also be submitted using the contact

    information below.

  </p>
