The Spot Sober Lounge

Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in plain language, how The Spot Sober Lounge mobile app (the “App”) handles information on iOS and Android.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, payment-card information, or other information that identifies you.

The App does not include third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics software development kits (SDKs).

Online Content and Network Access

The App connects to The Spot Sober Lounge website to retrieve and display public information such as meetings, events, merchandise, and membership details.

When this content is requested, the website and its hosting providers may automatically process routine technical information—such as an IP address, request time, requested resource, and basic device or network information—to deliver content, maintain reliability, and protect security. The App does not use this information to build user profiles or target advertising.

Information Stored on Your Device

To improve speed and keep previously retrieved content available when the website is slow or temporarily unavailable, the App may save copies of public meeting, event, product, and product-image content on your device.

This saved content is public website information, not information you submit. You can remove it by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. Because the App does not provide user accounts or maintain personal profiles, there is no App account data to delete.

External Websites and Purchases

The App may open third-party websites or services, including The Spot Sober Lounge website, YouTube, Google Maps, and external product or membership pages. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Merchandise and membership purchases are completed outside the App. The App does not directly process or store payment-card information.

Advertising and Tracking

The App does not display third-party advertising and does not use the Google Advertising ID or Apple Identifier for Advertisers for advertising, profiling, or ad targeting.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We limit the information handled by the App and use secure HTTPS connections to retrieve online content. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

  • You may choose not to open external links.
  • You may clear locally stored content through your device settings.
  • You may remove all locally stored App content by uninstalling the App.
  • You may contact us with any privacy or deletion question.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or applicable requirements change. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
