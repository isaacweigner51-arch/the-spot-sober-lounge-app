Privacy Policy for The Spot Sober Lounge App

Effective date: September 11, 2026

The Spot Sober Lounge respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the The Spot Sober Lounge mobile application handles information.

Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, or other personally identifiable information through the App.

App Content and Network Access

The App connects to The Spot Sober Lounge website to display current information such as meetings, events, merchandise, and membership information.

External Links

The App may open external websites or services, including The Spot Sober Lounge website, YouTube, Google Maps, and other third-party services. Those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Merchandise and membership purchases are completed outside the App through external websites. The App does not directly process or store payment card information.

Advertising

The App does not display third-party advertising and does not use the Google Advertising ID for advertising or ad targeting.

Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled by the App and to rely on secure connections when retrieving online content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
